Delivery & returns

Dispatched from Mussafah, Abu Dhabi, six days a week.

When will it arrive?

Abu Dhabi and Dubai: next working day on orders placed before 2pm. Northern Emirates: 2–3 working days. You get an SMS with the courier link the moment it leaves us.

What does delivery cost?

AED 20 standard, free once your bag passes AED 200. Next-day is AED 25 and can be chosen at checkout.

Can I pay cash?

Yes. Cash on delivery adds AED 5 and the courier carries a card machine too. Tabby splits any order into four payments.

What if the size is wrong?

Fourteen days to exchange, unworn with tags on. We collect from your door — no courier queue, no restocking fee.

How should I wash these?

30°C on a gentle cycle, inside out. Everything is pre-washed, so shrinkage after the first few washes stays under 3%.

First dibs on the next drop

One email a month: new sizes back in stock, and AED 25 off your first order over AED 150.
